Weekend and holiday rides

We ran a monthly Kidical Mass around River North through the summer of 2025 — a slower, family-paced weekend ride with music and bubbles, ending at a park. It hasn't run since, but we'd happily start it up again, and we're open to other one-off rides too: a trip to the lakefront, or somewhere worth pedalling to on a Saturday.

If you'd come to something like that, say so in the WhatsApp group. It takes about four families to make one worth organizing.
